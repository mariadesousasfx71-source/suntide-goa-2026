The festival

Three nights. Three stages. One shoreline.

SunTide brings house, techno, Bollywood bass and live indie acts to Vagator for the last week of the year. Dance from sunset to 1 a.m., then wake up to the Arabian Sea.

Sunset Sessions

Every evening opens with chilled live sets on the clifftop Horizon Stage.

Main Stage Nights

Headliners on the Tide Stage with a 360° light show by the sea.

Goan Food Village

Prawn rava fry, xacuti and bebinca from 30 local kitchens and shacks.

Leave No Trace

Reusable cups, refill stations and a beach clean-up every morning.

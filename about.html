About us

Made in Goa, for the sea

SunTide is a beach music festival run by a small Goan team who grew up dancing on these sands.

Our story

It started with a speaker on the sand

In 2024, a group of friends from Anjuna and Mapusa carried one speaker down to Vagator to watch the last sunset of the year. By midnight, two hundred people had joined them.

That night became SunTide. Our idea is simple: big music, local food and a clean beach the next morning. 2026 is our first full edition, with three stages and three nights by the Arabian Sea.

"We don't want people to just visit Goa for a weekend. We want them to feel it: the music, the food and the people."

Festival Director, SunTide Goa

What we stand for

Our three promises

Leave the beach better

No single-use plastic, refill stations at every stage and a volunteer clean-up every morning at 8 a.m.

Support local

30 Goan kitchens and shacks in the food village, and local crew, drivers and security staff.

A stage for Goa's sound

Every day, Goan bands and DJs play next to the headliners, not just before them.

FAQ

Good to know

Is there an age limit?

Yes. SunTide is 18+ only. Please bring a photo ID to the gate.

Can I buy a pass for one day only?

No. All passes cover all three days. See the passes on the home page.

Can I bring my own food and drinks?

Only an empty reusable bottle. You can fill it for free at the water refill stations.

How do I get to Vagator?

Festival shuttles run from Mapusa and Panaji every 30 minutes. Parking is for two-wheelers only.
